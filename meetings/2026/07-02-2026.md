## Presentation

"Multi-robot Air-Ground Collaboration with PX4 and Opportunistic Communications"

* [Fernando Cladera](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fcladera/)



### Notes

* Multi Robot Air-Ground collaboration with PX4 and opportunistic communications
* Information acquisition in large scale environments
    * locating objects of interest - car/person/building
    * executing tasks in groups
    * search and rescue, defense, precision agriculture
    * DARPA Triage challenge
* Heterogeneous teams of robots
    * Aerial - high altitude obstacle free motion, fast mapping, SWaP constraints
    * Ground - longer operation time, can approach objects of interest, but need to avoid obstacles, slow
* Aerial - RGB global shutter, orin nx, rajant dx2, f9p gnss, vn-100T IMU
* Ground - Ryzen5 3600, rtx4000, ouster OS1, camera
* Challenges - communication and coordination
    * SWaP platforms have restricted TX power yet high bandwidth needs
    * Central control with perfect state estimation - drone shows - 1 base station
    * how can robots communicate in adverserial settings, without centralized coordination?
    * SWaP constraints - Nvidia Thor - 2070 TFLOPS, devkit 3.65 kg
        * Google coral dual TPU - 8TOPS int8, 2W - 2.5g
        * VLMs, LLMs, and depth estimation needs to be tailored for onboard compute
   
* Mapping and semantics
    * UAV generates semantic map in real time -> Send to UGV for localization, find objects of interest, navigation
    * Dense metric-semantic maps, semantic classes are chosed during training
    * Localization can be made from semantic classes

* Opportunistic comms
    * Store and forward, gossip protocol
    * Drones act as physical carriers of messages from robot to base station, aerial platform acts as a mapping and comms relay
    * Ground robot motion not restricted by comms
    * Mesh networks - p2p radios, Rajant AG1 cardinal breadcrumb
    * Transparent layer 2 networking, faster association time and route convergence, API to get access to link metrics, channel redundancy and segmentation
    * Issues - no store and forward, easy to saturate link when 2 robots connect, multi hop increases channel utilization - DDS Storms
    * Key value db for comms
        * TX - goal selector, pose estimator, object mapper
        * message header for producer, timestamps, topic
        * wait for bandwidth of link to be better for sharing maps
        * sync happens when high quality link is detected
        * metrics (last communication, bandwidth) used to inform comm aware planner
        * Produced ROS Topic is used metrics to coordinate with ground stations
    * UAV operats in mission mode without autoContinue
    * Companion computer sends waypoints one at a time depending on the task, mavlink/mavros, qgc for viz/waypoints
        * PX4, QGC/MAVlink
        * Zenoh integration planning
    * Define preliminary QGC waypoints
        * geofence, heatmap, waypoint selection - LMPath
    * Air Router (Nimbus) - takes QGC plan file, transitions between exploration/communication based on the task the UAV is executing
    * waypoints to PX4 via mavlink
    * location of robot - last known location or last known goal
* Coordination - UGV goal allocation
    * Priority with pre-emption, robots with diff priority from pre-assigned lists
    * robots claim goals and communicate to other team members
    * maps improve as new communication with UAVs happen
* Extensions
    * increasing level of abstraction, classes are hardcoded during training
    * with foundational models, can now do zeroshot labels on the fly
    * SPINE planner
        * decompose mission as APIs, task sequencing and scene understanding
        * SPINE planning for single robot task
    * Mapping with zero-shot segmentation, labels requested by ground robot depending on mission requirements, sparse metric-semantic maps, reduces bandwidth requrements significantly
    * Multi robot coordination with LLMs, subtask generation with context of diff robots with diff capabilities

* Conclusion
    * Multi robot is hard
    * heterogenous capabilities fill gaps
